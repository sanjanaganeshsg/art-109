About Me

Hello! My name is Sanjana Ganesh, and I am currently pursuing a degree in Communication Studies at San Jose State University, with minors in Journalism and Digital Media Art. Through my work, I promote the ethical use of technology in storytelling. I aim to create projects that are not only unique and visually engaging but also functional and purposeful. I have worked as a Designer and Social Media Director for Redefine Z, a nonprofit organization, and as an Outreach Director for Bring Change to Mind (BC2M), a mental health club at my high school. I am currently expanding my skills in web design and look forward to continuing to grow as a creative professional.
